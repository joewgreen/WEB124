Metal Music

The History of Metal:

Metal, (or Heavy Metal), is a sub genre of rock music that features heavy and loud guitar, an emphatic rythm section, and vigorous vocals. The origin of the genre can be traced to louder rock bands of the late 1960's and early 1970's. A few of these pioneering bands include Led Zeppelin, Black Sabbath, and Deep Purple. They took the louder, more heavy elements of blues rock, psychadelic rock and acid rock and made this more aggresive rock sound their own. Now, nearly 60 years after the genre's inception, metal is one of the most popular sub genres of rock music.

Sub-Genres

What makes metal so captivating to me personally, are the many bands and sub genres that have expanded the genre further. Do you want louder and more aggresive guitar? Do you like an emphasis on thudding and energetic bass? Do you like music that is long, winding, and tells a story? There's absolutely a sub genre of metal that will scratch your itch.

A few of the most popular sub genres are:

  • Black metal: more of a creepy, haunting style of metal
  • Post metal: Longer, more drawn out songs with more of an emphasis on atmosphere than melody
  • Nu metal: Metal, or hard rock music mixed with hip hop
  • Glam metal: Metal with more pop sensabilites, not as agressive as other sub genres
  • Metalcore: Fuses metal with punk/hardcore punk music, often with odd time signatures and guitar playing.
